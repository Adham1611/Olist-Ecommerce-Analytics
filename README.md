🗝️ Executive Business Insights & KPI Summary
Total Revenue: ~$13.59M across ~112,650 orders.

Average Order Value (AOV): ~$120.65 per transaction.

Top Revenue States: São Paulo (SP), Rio de Janeiro (RJ), and Minas Gerais (MG) drive the majority of sales volume.

Leading Categories: Health & Beauty leads in total revenue generation, while Bed & Bath leads in overall units sold.

Payment Preference: Credit Card dominates payment methods with a 74% share, followed by Boleto.

Logistics Performance: Achieved a 97% successful delivery rate with less than 1% order cancellation/unavailability.

Customer Sentiment: 77.1% of customer reviews are highly positive (4–5 stars), maintaining an average review score of 4.09 / 5.00.
